I lead a team building an industrial IoT platform at Murrelektronik in Berlin, and build my own products on the side. There are two ways to work with me.

## Advisory work

Most teams have the AI tools by now. The harder part is what the tools change about the work: who checks what, how juniors still learn, what stays a person's job.

I know that shift from both sides. I spent ten years as a translator, in a profession AI rearranged from the inside. Today I lead a software team where the same thing is under way, and I build my own products with AI in them.

I talk this through with teams and the people leading them: where AI belongs in your work, how to agree on working with it, what to watch for with quality and with juniors. Start with a free 30-minute call. If there's more to do after that, we'll work out the shape together. More on how I think about this on the [about page](/about).

## Freelance projects

Websites and small web applications for small businesses, in German, English or Spanish. No templates: each one is built around what the business needs. I take on only a few projects at a time, so each one gets proper attention.

1. **Talk.** A free call of about 30 minutes. We work out what you need and whether I'm the right person for it.
2. **Proposal.** You get the scope, timeline and a fixed price in writing. No hourly meter.
3. **Build and launch.** You follow progress on a preview link. At launch everything is handed over to you, and I stay around to keep it running.

An example: [REB Consulting](/projects/reb-consulting), a Berlin real-estate agency whose website runs in five languages and shows listings live from their property software. For applications, see [Cadence](/projects/cadence) and [CV Tailor](/projects/cv-tailor).
