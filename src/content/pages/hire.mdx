I build websites for small businesses, in German, English or Spanish. No templates: each site is designed around what your business needs, and built to be fast, easy to find and easy to keep current.

I take on a small number of projects alongside my job leading a team at Murrelektronik, so each one gets proper attention.

## What you get

- A design made for your business, not a theme
- A site that loads fast and works on every device
- The basics for being found: structure, titles, a Google Business profile
- Help with the text, plus imprint and privacy pages

## How it works

1. **Talk.** A free call of about 30 minutes. We work out what you need and whether I'm the right person for it.
2. **Proposal.** You get the scope, timeline and a fixed price in writing. No hourly meter.
3. **Build and launch.** 50% up front, then I start. You follow progress on a preview link, with revision rounds agreed in the proposal. The remaining 50% is due before launch, and then everything is handed over to you.

## An example

[REB Consulting](/projects/reb-consulting) is a Berlin real-estate agency. Their new website runs in five languages and shows listings live from their property software, so nothing is typed in twice.

## Price

The price is fixed and agreed after the call. Websites start at €2,500; most complete projects land between €4,000 and €7,000, depending on content, languages and features.

The first year of care is included. After that, €50 a month covers hosting, domain, technical updates and small content changes.
