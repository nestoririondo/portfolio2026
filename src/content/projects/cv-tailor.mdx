## What it does

You import your CV once into a reusable profile. CV Tailor searches for jobs that match it, scores how well you fit each one, and generates a CV tailored to the posting. You edit the draft, export a PDF that applicant tracking systems can read, and track your applications in one place.

## How it treats AI output

The profile is the source of facts. Everything the AI produces is a suggestion that needs review. Generating a CV creates a draft application; sending it is a separate step that only you take.

## How it is built

A Next.js application with one SQLite database per account, Claude for import, tailoring and matching, and headless Chromium for reading job pages and exporting PDFs.
